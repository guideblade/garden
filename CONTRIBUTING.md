# Contributing

Issues and suggestions are welcome.

Open an issue if:

- An extension in a pack is causing problems.
- You want to add a new extension to a sub-pack.
- You think an extension does not belong in a sub-pack.
- You have an idea, question, concern, or anything else related to the packs.

Discussion is encouraged. Feedback is useful, even when it does not result in a change.

These packs are opinionated. I make the final decision on what gets added, removed, moved, renamed, or otherwise changed.

My goal is to make useful, portable packs without too much quirk. I want them to work across multiple machines and different projects. I also want them to be useful to colleagues and other people. I just got tired of setting of downloading all the same ones over and over.

## Pull Requests

Pull requests are welcome, especially when they are small and focused.

For extension list changes, include:

- The extension name and identifier.
- Which pack or sub-pack should change.
- Why the extension belongs there, or why it should be removed.

For structural changes, explain the intent. Keep unrelated formatting or cleanup out of the same pull request.
